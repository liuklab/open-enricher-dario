Imperial probe droids scour the galaxy for the Rebels. On the ice planet Hoth, Luke Skywalker sees what he takes for a meteor before a wampa knocks him from his mount. He escapes the creature's cave but collapses in the snow. Obi-Wan appears to him and tells him to seek out Yoda on Dagobah. Han Solo finds Luke and keeps him alive until a rescue patrol arrives. An Imperial probe has meanwhile discovered the Rebel base. As Imperial walkers advance, Luke and the other pilots buy time for the transports to escape.

Luke takes R2-D2 to Dagobah. The small stranger who tests his patience turns out to be Yoda. When Luke gives up on lifting his submerged X-wing, Yoda raises it from the swamp. Elsewhere, Han, Leia, Chewbacca and C-3PO flee Hoth aboard the Millennium Falcon. Its hyperdrive fails. Han hides among asteroids, where he and Leia grow closer, then seeks repairs from his old friend Lando Calrissian on Cloud City. Boba Fett has tracked the Falcon there. Vader has already forced Lando into turning over his guests.

Vader uses Han's suffering to draw Luke to the city. Luke sees his friends in distress and leaves before his training is complete, despite warnings from Yoda and Obi-Wan. Leia tells Han she loves him before he is frozen in carbonite to test the chamber Vader intends for Luke. Lando helps Leia and Chewbacca escape, but Fett gets away with Han, bound for Jabba the Hutt.

Luke faces Vader above a deep shaft. Vader cuts off his right hand, tells him he is his father and invites him to rule at his side. Luke lets himself fall. Hanging beneath Cloud City, he reaches out to Leia, who has the Falcon turn back for him. R2-D2 restores the hyperdrive and they escape. Aboard a Rebel medical ship, Luke receives an artificial hand. He and Leia watch as Lando and Chewbacca depart to find Han.
