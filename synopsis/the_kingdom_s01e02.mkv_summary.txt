After presidential candidate Armando Badajoz is stabbed at a campaign rally, his running mate, Pastor Emilio Vázquez Pena, gives his account to prosecutor Roberta Candia. Still shaken and bloodstained, Emilio acknowledges that he knows the suspect, Remigio Cárdenas, through his church. He believes Remigio had changed since finding religion and struggles to understand why he would kill Badajoz. He declines police protection.

Julio Clamens confronts Tadeo about the weapons in Remigio's room. Julio suspects someone paid Remigio, but Tadeo directs him to Remigio himself. At the church, Elena moves to keep the family together and sensitive papers are shredded in a concealed room. She urges Emilio to see the possibility of taking Badajoz's place on the ticket as a calling from God.

Candia reviews surveillance footage and questions whether disturbances that drew security away from the rally were coordinated. The case takes a different turn when Remigio says he stabbed Pastor Emilio, who is still alive. He insists that Julio Clamens represent him. Emilio closes the episode in prayer, reflecting on Christ's cry of abandonment and pledging to follow God's will.
