During Andy's childhood nine years earlier, R.C. (a remote-controlled car) is being swept down a storm drain. Woody leads a successful rescue operation with the help of other toys.
While they are busy, Bo Peep and her sheep are given away by Andy's younger sister, Molly. Despite Woody's efforts to convince her to stay, Bo reassures him that part of being a toy is being taken away. Woody briefly considers going with her, but after realizing Andy still needs him, they bid each other farewell.

When Andy leaves for college, he gives his toys to Bonnie. Woody often finds himself left in the closet while Bonnie pins his sheriff's badge on Jessie. As kindergarten approaches, Bonnie is anxious and her father tells her she cannot take a toy to orientation.

Worried that Bonnie will feel overwhelmed at her kindergarten orientation, Woody, whom Bonnie has recently been neglecting, sneaks into her backpack. Bonnie is nervous and almost cries after a classmate discards her arts and crafts supplies. Woody covertly recovers some of the materials from the trash, along with a Spork. Bonnie turns the Spork into a handmade toy she names "Forky". Forky comes to life in Bonnie's backpack, shocking Woody.

At Bonnie's house, Forky experiences an existential crisis, believing that he was made to be trash and not a toy. With Forky having become Bonnie's favorite toy, Woody has to continually prevent him from throwing himself away. Forky is not very well constructed. He has 2 eyes each of different sizes, his arms won't stay steady around his body and keep falling down and Forky is very unstable on his feet, stumbling every time he walks even a few feet. Woody says that Bonnie had the biggest smile on her face while playing with Forky and is convinced that the new toy is key to Bonnie adjusting to kindergarten.
Meanwhile, Forky is terrified and finds even Bonnie to be scary.

On Bonnie's family's road trip, the family is travelling in an RV and Bonnie brings many of her toys along. Forky keeps trying to jump out, while Woody keeps bringing him back. Forky jumps out of the window and Woody follows him out. Woody convinces Forky of the joys of being a toy, and they journey to the RV park in a nearby town where Bonnie and her parents will be staying. Woody says that Forky will help Bonnie make memories that will stay with her for the rest of her life.
As Woody talks, Forky realizes that Woody was Andy's toy and says that now Woody is also trash. Forky says that trash makes him feel warm, cozy and safe. Woody explains to Forky that Bonnie also feels warm and cozy when she is with Forky.

Forky begins to take pride in being Bonnie's toy. While heading towards the RV park where Bonnie's family is staying, Woody spots Bo's lamp in an antique store window. Searching for Bo inside, he and Forky encounter a doll named Gabby Gabby and her sycophant ventriloquist's dummies, the Bensons.

Gabby offers to take them to Bo but soon reveals her true plan to obtain Woody's voice box, since her own is broken and no child will purchase her. Gabby had already ascertained that Woody was made around the same time in the 1950s that she was, and hence their parts might be compatible with each other.
Woody flees with Forky, but they are separated. Woody escapes from the dummies, but Gabby captures Forky. By the time Woody escapes the shop, he realizes that he has left Forky behind.

At a playground, Woody reunites with Bo and her sheep Billy, Goat, and Gruff. Bo now lives the free lifestyle of a "lost" toy, but she agrees to help Woody save Forky and get back to Bonnie.

Meanwhile, Buzz searches for Woody on his own, seeking guidance from his inner voice, the button-induced phrases from his own voice box. Buzz finds himself as a carnival prize in a fairground between the RV park and the antique store. He escapes with plush toys Ducky and Bunny. They meet up with Woody and Bo. Along with pocket toy cop Giggle McDimples and Canadian stuntman toy Duke Caboom, they try but again fail to rescue Forky from Gabby Gabby. The store owner's cat nearly kills Giggle during the attempt.

Despite Woody's efforts to convince the group to try another rescue attempt, Bo, unwilling to risk the group's lives, angrily leaves with the others after Woody indirectly insults her for not having a kid to look after. Woody encounters Gabby Gabby again, who tells him of her longing to belong to the store owner's granddaughter, Harmony. Woody gives her his voice box in exchange for Forky.

Buzz gets Bonnie to go back for the backpack she left at the antique store. Harmony finally tries Gabby's repaired voice box but leaves the doll behind. Woody sends Forky back to Bonnie with a message for Buzz to meet him at the carousel. He stays to comfort Gabby and invites her to come with them. Bo and her friends return to help. Trixie imitates the RV's navigation system while the other toys steer Bonnie's family toward the rendezvous.

When Gabby sees a crying girl lost in the carnival, she decides to become that child's toy. Taking responsibility for Gabby, the girl approaches a security guard and is reunited with her parents. The girl takes Gabby with her.

At the carousel, Woody and Bo share a bittersweet goodbye. Realizing Woody feels torn, Buzz tells him that Bonnie will be okay without him. Deciding to stay with Bo, Woody embraces Bonnie's toys farewell and gives Jessie his sheriff badge. The RV departs, and Bonnie's toys leave with her.

As the carnival travels, Woody and Bo help prize toys find children. Ducky and Bunny imagine using superpowers in another of their elaborate fantasies.

In Bonnie's room, mirroring the scene where Woody introduces Forky, Jessie brings home a new friend Bonnie created: Karen Beverly, a decorated plastic knife. Instantly smitten, Forky offers to shepherd her in her journey through life as a toy instead of trash.

Duke Caboom successfully jumps onto the 'I' of the Pixar logo, poses, and gives the winter Combat Carl a long-awaited high five.
